## Key ideas encountered this month

- HPL2 argues learning is inseparable from cultural context and developmental stage — not just a cognitive event. This reframes what "teaching CS" means at a faith-based institution.
- Dehnadi & Bornat's "camel" claim (mental model consistency predicts success) is contested — but the underlying question (what does a student bring into CS1?) is the right one. Prior knowledge is real and consequential.
- Guzdial's learner-centered framework directly challenges the "teach the language, they'll figure out the rest" approach dominant in intro CS.
